    1. Scope and Regulatory Authority

    This document establishes the binding protocols, statutory compliance guidelines, and administrative procedures enforced across all digital portals, scholarly monograph repositories, student registries, and physical research infrastructure maintained by the AcademyVision Institute for Higher Learning ("AcademyVision", "we", "us", or "our").

    By engaging with AcademyVision digital services, reading research archives, or enrolling in academic curricula, scholars, matriculated students, visiting researchers, and members of the public explicitly consent to the standards described herein.

    2. Data Custodianship & Student Educational Records (FERPA/GDPR)

    All student educational records, research preprints, transcripts, and faculty evaluations are safeguarded in accordance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and relevant state educational privacy statutes.

    AcademyVision does not monetize, sell, or license student or researcher data to third-party commercial entities. Data generated through computational learning laboratories is fully anonymized and deployed solely for verified epistemological and pedagogical enhancement.

    3. Academic Integrity & Intellectual Property Rights

    All intellectual property developed through sponsored university research, doctoral theses, and institutional monographs remains governed by the AcademyVision Patent & Intellectual Property Charter. While open-access publications are freely accessible for scholarly quotation and educational non-commercial dissemination, commercial reproduction requires express written permission from the Academic Council.

    4. Telemetry, Cookies, and Digital Authentication

    AcademyVision utilizes secure session cookies, encryption tokens, and telemetry analytics solely to maintain authenticated scholar portals, measure institutional monograph access patterns, and defend our computing infrastructure against distributed denial-of-service vulnerabilities. Scholars may manage cookie preferences via their browser settings.

    5. Amendments and Institutional Inquiries

    The Academic Council reserves the prerogative to amend these terms periodically in alignment with statutory revisions and pedagogical governance updates. Continued usage of AcademyVision services subsequent to modifications constitutes ratification of the updated covenant.

    Inquiries regarding privacy rights, data rectification, or academic licensing should be transmitted to our registry at contact@academyvision.com.
